LegendaryAnimals.

Creation of a trio of outlaws ahead of their time. A virtual music group turned animated series.

02 Image Reel

3 Stars were born.

The largest project I've ever been contracted for - to create a virtual hip-hop music group like an outlaw version of Gorillaz. Once the project was completed after 6 months of work, including learning Unreal Engine, I fell in love with the characters and joined the group as the main producer of content of all content regarding the group to keep my vision's integrity.

What did it take?

Learning Unreal Engine (and relearning Blender) meant I had to figure out hundreds of new skills before I even began to attempt animating any scene. Even after using facial mocap and rigging to base UE skeletons, the learning curve sometimes meant spending a week figuring out a single scene or physics control function.
