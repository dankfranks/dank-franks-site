Dark/Light Mode

Easier Said Than Done.

Formatting for Lottie is much more technical than typical animating workflow in After Effects. The export is code, so it's mandatory to optimize and aim for a single shape layer if possible to prevent bogging down website. Certain techniques must be used, such as grouping multiple paths with the same appearance with a single fill/stroke color, never duplicating.
